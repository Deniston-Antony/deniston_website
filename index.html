I enjoy building robots, developing creative projects, and exploring technology to turn ideas into something real. For me, making things is not just about solving problems; it is about curiosity, experimentation, and learning something new along the way.

Beyond technology, I have a deep appreciation for Tamil poetry and literature. I write poems in Tamil and collect or create meaningful quotes in both Tamil and English, finding beauty in simple words and everyday experiences.

I also care deeply about nature and the world around us. Whether it is observing plants, appreciating the outdoors, or working on projects inspired by nature, I believe technology and creativity should exist alongside the natural world.
