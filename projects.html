Project one

3D Point loud Plant Phenotyping

project-summary-one

Developed a 3D point-cloud-based plant phenotyping pipeline for extracting the structural information of individual plants. The workflow involves point-cloud preprocessing, stem–leaf segmentation, k-NN graph construction, stem centerline extraction, leaf clustering, stem–leaf junction detection, and leaf midrib extraction. The resulting plant skeleton is smoothed and represented as a graph-based plant structure, enabling measurements of plant traits such as stem and leaf geometry for further phenotypic analysis.

Automated extraction and analysis of plant structure from 3D point clouds to identify stems, leaves, and their geometric characteristics.
